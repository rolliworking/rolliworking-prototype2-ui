**One paragraph.** A finished watch leaves the shop only through five gates, each written as a fact into `SalesOrder.pickupDraft` and ALL re-validated by `confirmPickup()` at the moment of release (the UI mirrors `pickupBlockers()`, it never decides). 1 **Item** — the counter staffer compares the intake photos with the piece on the counter (Same item / Not the same item → logged stop + MH pin). 2 **Invoice** — balance must be $0; the SOLE exception is a bypass approved by a *different* manager (their PIN + reason), stamped on the SO and fed to MH's accountability feed. 3 **Verify** — QR scan (`RSPU:<SO>:<code>`), typed code, proxy (name + government-ID photo + a different manager), or reverse QR (station shows a single-use 2-minute token; the client's phone opens `/rc/pickup/:token` and taps Yes / That's not me). Every resend (email or SMS) rotates the code; stale generations are refused with "replaced on …". 4 **Photos** — intake and hand-back crops side by side, Claude vision reads both serials in one call, three-way compare against the record names the failing pair; `unreadable` → retake, `mismatch` → hard stop unless a different manager overrides with a reason. 5 **Complete** — Done needs frame 1 from the `cameraRole: 'client'` camera (or a manager camera bypass); frames 2–6 arrive over 60 s; short after 90 s → `incomplete` + MH pin. One `PickupSession` record drives the SO card, the job-timeline close row and the Client 360 custody row. Signature-free (locked). Retention recorded on the session: frames 90 d · proxy ID 30 d · summary permanent (enforcement = KEEPER).
