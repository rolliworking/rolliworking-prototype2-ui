## 1. Vocabulary
- **Vendor** (`Vendor` with `kind: 'parts' | 'outsource'`, `country`, `ships: boolean`, payment terms) — one **lane** per outsource vendor. Lane shape by "do we ship to them?": `SHIP_LANE` = In queue → Sent → At vendor / In progress → Inbound → Received (7 cells incl. redo stages); `NOSHIP_LANE` = 4 cells (hand-off, no transit). `laneStagesFor(vendor)`.
- **SWO line** (`Swo`) = one job's components + the work asked (`components: ComponentKey[]`, `work`, `stage: SwoStage`, `paid` flag independent of stage, `vendorInvoiceTotal`, `qboStatus`, `predictedCompletion`, `outbound / returnLabel: SwoLabel`, `redoCycles`, `vendorReplies`, `statusRequests`, `sentParts`, `hubId`).
- **SWO hub** (`SwoHub`) = the **container** (box / envelope / hand-off batch) that goes to a vendor: `number`, `barcode`, `vendorId`, `pointPerson`, `predictedCompletion`, `invoices`, `redoCycles`, `shipments: SwoShipment[]`, `internalNotes`, `printedAt`; `SwoHubView` adds vendor, lines, derived `stage`, open / back / total, international, paid, `sentSummary`. Lines move between hubs (`moveSwoLines`, `moveLineToHub`, `openHubsForVendor`); the hub barcode scans on the Assign map (`findHubByCode`, `isSwoCode`).
- **Health** (`concierge.ts swoHealth → SwoHealth = fulfilled | returning | breach | delayed | at_risk | on_track | queued | inspection`), **escalation level** (`escalationLevel`, `syncConciergeAlerts` → inbox rows), **component-wait chips** (`componentWaitsSync` → `ComponentWaitChips` on pads, job detail, jobs board, hitlist), **outstanding** (`getVendorOutstanding`, `OutstandingRow`).
